## Published

**[Towards Trustless Academic Records in Higher Education](https://doi.org/10.1109/3ICT68299.2025.11442139)**
Peer-reviewed at IEEE 3ICT 2025. Blockchain and IPFS for verifiable student credentials. I engineered
the permissioned Hyperledger Besu network, the verifiable-credential contracts with role-based access
control, and the IPFS storage layer. *3rd of 9 authors.*

**[34 open models on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/nahiar)**
Spam, hate speech and abusive language, sentiment, emotion, NER, topic modelling, language
identification and social bot detection — most of them for Indonesian, Javanese, Sundanese and
neighbouring languages, where good open models are thin on the ground. Grouped into collections:
[NLP Indonesia Multitask](https://huggingface.co/collections/nahiar/nlp-indonesia-multitask-687f0c565d19acbd3c5e6393)
and [Bot Detection](https://huggingface.co/collections/nahiar/bot-detection-6926b38366ae2cc21c4e62a9).

**[nahiarhdNLP](https://pypi.org/project/nahiarhdNLP/)** · [source](https://github.com/nahiarhd/NLP_nahiarhd)
An Indonesian natural language processing library on PyPI, 34 releases and counting. MIT.

**[fastapi-project-creator](https://pypi.org/project/fastapi-project-creator/)**
A pip-installable CLI that scaffolds a FastAPI project — folder layout, settings, routers — in one
command, so the first commit is the feature rather than the boilerplate. MIT.
